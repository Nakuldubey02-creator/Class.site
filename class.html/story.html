The Edible Cup Business Pitch

An edible cup business turns environmental waste into a delicious, zero-waste snacking experience by replacing single-use plastic and paper with crunchy, grain-based containers.

💡 The Core Brand Story: "Sip, Crunch, Repeat"

The Hook: Imagine finishing your morning masala chai or artisanal coffee and instead of throwing away a soggy paper cup, you simply take a satisfying bite out of the cup itself.

The Problem: Millions of single-use paper and plastic cups choke our landfills daily. Even paper cups contain hidden plastic linings that prevent easy recycling.

The Epiphany: Sustainability shouldn't feel like a chore; it should be integrated seamlessly into daily life by making the container part of the treat.

The Solution: Crafting sturdy, delicious cups out of native grains like ragi (finger millet), rice flour, and oats that hold hot liquids for over 30 minutes without leaking, then taste like a crisp cookie afterward.

📊 Business Model & Unit Economics

Metric Details
Manufacturing Cost ₹6 to ₹8 per cup (using basic baking or semi-automatic molding equipment).
B2B Selling Price ₹20 to ₹30 per unit when supplying premium cafes and zero-waste event planners.
Target Channels High-footfall coffee chains, wedding caterers, tech-park cafeterias, and D2C markets.

🗓 Phased Execution Plan

  1. Phase 1: R&D & Recipe Locking
    • Test grain ratios to ensure structural integrity with hot liquids for 30+ minutes.
    • Introduce mild flavors like vanilla, chocolate, or plain roasted grain to complement tea and coffee.
  2. Phase 2: Local B2B Launch
    • Partner with 5 to 10 local cafes or corporate cafeterias as exclusive "Zero-Waste Pioneers."
    • Provide branded display dispensers that highlight the "Drink the Tea, Eat the Cup" concept to drive engagement.
  3. Phase 3: Scaling & Automation
    • Invest in semi-automatic or industrial cup-making machinery as demand grows.
    • Expand distribution into large-scale event catering and wedding planners.
