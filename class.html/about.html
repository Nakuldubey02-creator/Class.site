Our Mission

Millions of disposable coffee cups end up in landfills every single day. We set out to change that by creating a cup that doesn't just disappear—it tastes good too.

Our edible coffee cups are engineered from 100% natural, vegan ingredients. They offer a sturdy, crunchy, and delicious alternative to single-use plastics without altering the authentic flavor profile of your brew.
